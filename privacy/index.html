Privacy Policy for Vitto App

Last updated: 3 de setembro de 2026.

Vitto App is a web-based content workflow tool used to prepare, review, and publish content to TikTok accounts authorized by the account owner through TikTok's official APIs, including Login Kit and Content Posting API where approved.

1. Who we are

Vitto App is operated as a small web app for owned or explicitly authorized TikTok content workflows. The app is not designed to collect TikTok passwords or operate accounts without authorization.

2. Data we may process

Depending on permissions granted by the TikTok user and enabled features, we may process technical and content-related data required to operate the service, such as TikTok account identifiers, display information returned by the TikTok API, OAuth authorization status, post captions, media URLs, publication status, timestamps, and logs needed for deduplication, troubleshooting, and audit.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect TikTok passwords, payment information, government identification documents, private messages, or sensitive personal data that is not required for the app's stated functionality.

4. Authentication and tokens

Authentication is performed through TikTok's official OAuth/Login Kit flow. Access tokens are used only to perform actions authorized by the user and are stored in protected automation credentials or secure server-side configuration. Tokens are not sold or shared.

5. How we use TikTok API data

API data is used to identify the authorized account, test sandbox functionality, and publish or manage content explicitly prepared by the account owner or authorized operator. The app requests only the scopes necessary for the enabled features.

6. Affiliate and commercial content

Some content prepared through the app may include affiliate links or commercial recommendations. When applicable, posts should include clear disclosure such as #publi and a notice that price and availability may change.

7. Sharing of data

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by hosting, automation, storage, analytics, and official API providers necessary to run the app. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or platform policy enforcement.

8. Retention and deletion

Technical records are retained only as long as needed for app operation, security, audit, troubleshooting, and duplicate prevention. Users may request deletion of app-related records through the official contact channel displayed on the app page.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including official OAuth flows, server-side token storage, access limitation, and avoiding public exposure of credentials or secrets.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app, TikTok API requirements, legal obligations, or platform policies. The latest version will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions about Vitto App, use the official contact channel displayed on the app home page.
